# matsci-inv

**Computational materials science, nanomaterials, spin dynamics, and electromagnetics research.**

## About

This repository serves as a portfolio and collection of research code, 
analysis workflows, and computational projects in materials science and 
nanotechnology.

My work combines atomistic simulation, numerical analysis, and 
electromagnetic modeling to investigate the structure–property 
relationships of nanoscale materials and devices.

## Research Areas

- Atomistic spin dynamics and magnonics
- Defect-mediated magnetic dynamics
- Nanostructured and functional materials
- RF and electromagnetic metamaterials
- Flexible and wearable devices
- Computational microscopy and diffraction

## Selected Projects

### Atomistic CoFe/CoFe:Cu Spin Dynamics

Atomistic spin-dynamics simulations of defect-mediated redistribution 
of ferromagnetic-resonance spectral weight in CoFe/CoFe:Cu multilayers.

**Methods:** VAMPIRE, atomistic LLG dynamics, temporal FFT, spatial 
Fourier analysis, \(k\)-space mode decomposition.

**Key analysis:**
- FMR frequency extraction
- \(k\)-space spectral-weight mapping
- Defect and roughness quantification
- 3×3 Cu-concentration/roughness parameter sweep
- Mode-resolved analysis of finite-wavevector excitations

### Flexible RF Metamaterials

Metamaterial-inspired geometric structuring for deformation-stable 
flexible RF antennas.

**Methods:** electromagnetic simulation, impedance/S-parameter analysis,
geometric optimization, and experimental characterization.

> Project repository coming soon.

### Graphene Grain-Boundary Analysis

Computational analysis of graphene grain-boundary structures and their 
electron-diffraction response.

**Methods:** atomistic structural analysis, simulated diffraction, 
graph/network analysis, and numerical visualization.
